# XSpan Contribute — Research Partner Access Agreement

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** [DATE]

---

This Research Partner Access Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between **XSpan, Inc.** ("XSpan") and the Research Partner entity ("Research Partner") identified during Contribute Program registration.

---

## 1. Purpose

This Agreement governs the Research Partner's access to and use of De-Identified Datasets obtained through Research Access Grants via the XSpan Contribute Program (the "Research Data Contribution Program").

---

## 2. Definitions

- **"De-Identified Dataset"** — A dataset that has been processed through XSpan's HIPAA Safe Harbor de-identification pipeline, meeting the requirements of 45 CFR 164.514(b)(2), with k-anonymity (k >= 5) verification.
- **"Data Contributor"** — The individual who created and listed the De-Identified Dataset.
- **"Research Access Grant"** — The license obtained by a Research Partner to access and use a De-Identified Dataset for Permitted Purposes.
- **"Permitted Purpose"** — Drug development, clinical trial design, biomedical research, population health analysis, medical device development, health technology development, public health surveillance, or other lawful research and commercial purposes declared during Research Partner registration.

---

## 3. Permitted Uses

Research Partner may use De-Identified Datasets obtained through Research Access Grants solely for:

3.1. Biomedical and clinical research
3.2. Drug and therapeutic development
3.3. Clinical trial design and patient cohort analysis
3.4. Population health analytics and epidemiological studies
3.5. Medical device development and validation
3.6. Health technology product development
3.7. Public health surveillance and reporting
3.8. Machine learning model training for healthcare applications
3.9. Other lawful purposes declared during registration and approved by XSpan

---

## 4. Prohibited Uses

Research Partner shall NOT:

4.1. **Re-identification**: Attempt to identify, directly or indirectly, any individual Data Contributor from a De-Identified Dataset, whether alone or in combination with any other data source, dataset, public record, or information.

4.2. **Contact attempts**: Attempt to contact, locate, or communicate with any individual whose data may be represented in a De-Identified Dataset.

4.3. **Insurance underwriting**: Use De-Identified Datasets to make or inform individual insurance coverage, pricing, or eligibility decisions.

4.4. **Employment decisions**: Use De-Identified Datasets to make or inform individual hiring, termination, or employment eligibility decisions.

4.5. **Law enforcement**: Provide De-Identified Datasets to law enforcement agencies or use them for surveillance, investigation, or prosecution of individuals.

4.6. **Discriminatory purposes**: Use De-Identified Datasets in any manner that would result in unlawful discrimination against individuals or groups.

4.7. **Unauthorized redistribution**: Resell, sublicense, redistribute, or make publicly available any De-Identified Dataset, in whole or in part, to any third party without prior written consent from XSpan.

4.8. **Data linking for re-identification**: Merge, link, or combine De-Identified Datasets with identified datasets, voter registration records, social media data, or any other dataset for the purpose of re-identifying individuals.

---

## 5. Data Security Requirements

Research Partner shall:

5.1. Store De-Identified Datasets in encrypted storage (AES-256 or equivalent) with access controls limiting access to authorized personnel.

5.2. Maintain an access log recording all personnel who access De-Identified Datasets.

5.3. Implement technical safeguards consistent with industry standards (SOC 2, ISO 27001, or equivalent).

5.4. Report any suspected data breach, unauthorized access, or re-identification incident to XSpan within 72 hours of discovery at security@xspan.ai.

5.5. Destroy De-Identified Datasets upon request if Research Partner's Contribute Program access is terminated for violation of this Agreement.

---

## 6. Research Publications

6.1. Research Partner may publish research findings derived from De-Identified Datasets in peer-reviewed journals, conference proceedings, and other scientific publications.

6.2. Publications must cite the XSpan Research Data Contribution Program as the data source.

6.3. Publications must NOT include any data at a granularity that could enable re-identification of individual Data Contributors (e.g., a single record with a unique combination of rare biomarker values).

6.4. Research Partner agrees to provide XSpan with copies of publications derived from Contribute Program data for archival purposes.

---

## 7. Verification and Audit Rights

7.1. XSpan reserves the right to audit Research Partner's compliance with this Agreement upon 30 days' written notice.

7.2. Research Partner shall make relevant records, systems, and personnel available for audit.

7.3. If an audit reveals a material breach, Research Partner shall bear the reasonable costs of the audit.

---

## 8. Term and Termination

8.1. This Agreement is effective upon Research Partner's first Research Access Grant and remains in effect as long as Research Partner retains any De-Identified Datasets.

8.2. XSpan may terminate this Agreement immediately upon discovery of a material breach, including but not limited to any re-identification attempt.

8.3. Upon termination for cause, Research Partner shall certify destruction of all De-Identified Datasets within 30 days.

8.4. Sections 4 (Prohibited Uses), 5 (Data Security), and 9 (Indemnification) survive termination.

---

## 9. Indemnification

Research Partner shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless XSpan, its officers, employees, and Data Contributors from any claims, damages, losses, or expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from:

9.1. Research Partner's breach of this Agreement
9.2. Research Partner's misuse of De-Identified Datasets
9.3. Any re-identification incident caused by Research Partner's actions or negligence
9.4. Any third-party claims arising from Research Partner's use of accessed data

---

10.2. XSpan does not warrant the accuracy, completeness, or clinical validity of De-Identified Datasets. Data reflects self-reported, device-measured, and EHR-derived values as collected by individual Data Contributors.

10.3. XSpan's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total amount paid by Research Partner for Research Access Grants in the 12 months preceding the claim.
