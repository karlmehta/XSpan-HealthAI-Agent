## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how XSpan, Inc. ("XSpan," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with XSpan Contribute ("Contribute Program"). This policy supplements the XSpan HealthAI Agent Privacy Policy and applies specifically to Contribute Program activities.

---

## 2. Our Privacy Architecture

XSpan is designed with a privacy-first architecture. Understanding what data lives where is critical:

| Data Type | Where It Lives | Who Can Access |
|-----------|---------------|----------------|
| Raw health data (PHI) | Your local device only (~/.xspan/data) | Only you |
| Synthesized biomarker vectors | Your device + XSpan Cloud (Pro users) | You + XSpan HIPAA-compliant systems |
| De-Identified Datasets (Contribute Program) | Encrypted secure storage | You + verified Research Partners who obtain access |
| Contribution listing metadata | Secure digital ledger (public) | Anyone (but contains no health values) |
| Wallet address | Secure digital ledger (public) | Anyone (pseudonymous, not linked to identity) |
| Account information (email, name) | XSpan servers | XSpan staff with legitimate need |
| Contribution rewards records | XSpan servers + secure digital ledger | You + XSpan + ledger (pseudonymous) |

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Information You Provide
- Account registration details (name, email address)
- Wallet address
- Contribute Program preferences (pricing, data category selections)
- Consent records (versions accepted, timestamps)
- Support communications

### 3.2 Information Generated by the Contribute Program
- De-Identified Dataset metadata (completeness score, category tags, demographic bucket — age range, sex, zip3)
- Transaction records (listings created, Research Access Grants, contribution rewards)
- Digital ledger transaction records and event logs

### 3.3 Information We Do NOT Collect
- Your raw health data (PHI) — this never leaves your device
- Health values or biomarker readings — only de-identified, generalized data is uploaded to encrypted storage by your Agent
- Your device's local database (~/.xspan/data/xspan.db)
- Passwords for connected health services (stored in your OS keychain only)

## 4. HIPAA De-Identification

All data shared through the Contribute Program is de-identified using the HIPAA Safe Harbor method (45 CFR 164.514(b)(2)). The following 18 identifiers are removed or generalized **locally on your device** before any data leaves:

| # | Identifier | Handling |
|---|-----------|---------|
| 1 | Names | Never collected in biomarker data |
| 2 | Geographic data (below state) | Generalized to ZIP3; suppressed if population < 20,000 |
| 3 | Dates (except year) | Replaced with relative day offsets from random epoch |
| 4 | Phone numbers | Not collected |
| 5 | Fax numbers | Not collected |
| 6 | Email addresses | Not included in datasets |
| 7 | Social Security numbers | Not collected |
| 8 | Medical record numbers | Stripped; replaced with random dataset ID |
| 9 | Health plan beneficiary numbers | Not collected |
| 10 | Account numbers | Not included |
| 11 | Certificate/license numbers | Not collected |
| 12 | Vehicle identifiers | Not collected |
| 13 | Device identifiers | Stripped from all records |
| 14 | Web URLs | Not included |
| 15 | IP addresses | Not included |
| 16 | Biometric identifiers | HRV patterns receive Laplacian noise (epsilon=1.0) |
| 17 | Full-face photographs | Not collected |
| 18 | Any unique identifying number | All UUIDs and user IDs stripped |

### Additional Privacy Measures
- **k-Anonymity (k >= 5)**: Your demographic bucket (age range + sex + zip3) must match at least 4 other Contribute Program participants. If not, fields are further generalized.
- **Differential Privacy**: Calibrated Laplacian noise is applied to continuous biomarker values that could serve as quasi-identifiers.
- **Temporal De-correlation**: Snapshot ordering is randomized; day offsets receive +/- 1-3 day jitter.

## 6. Information Sharing

### 6.1 With Research Partners
- Only De-Identified Datasets are shared, and only after a Research Access Grant is obtained and payment is confirmed.
- Research Partners never receive your name, email, account information, or wallet-to-identity mapping.
- Research Partners receive encrypted data from secure storage and a decryption key specific to their access grant.

### 6.2 With Health System Partners
- If you were acquired through a Health System Partner, we share only: (a) that you are an active Contribute Program participant, and (b) aggregate contribution rewards statistics for their referred users.
- We never share your individual contribution rewards, dataset contents, or listing details with Health System Partners.

### 6.3 With Service Providers
- Encrypted storage services — store encrypted, de-identified data only
- Secure digital ledger — public metadata (no health values)
- Payment processors (for bank withdrawal) — financial information only

### 6.4 Legal Obligations
We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.

## 7. Digital Ledger and Public Data

Certain Contribute Program data is recorded on a secure digital ledger and is publicly visible:

- **Visible on the ledger**: Dataset listing metadata (category tags, completeness score, demographic bucket, price), wallet addresses (pseudonymous), consent record hashes, transaction records.
- **NOT visible on the ledger**: Your identity, email, health values, raw data, wallet-to-identity mapping.

Digital ledger data is immutable by nature. Once a transaction is recorded, it cannot be deleted. However, the ledger data contains no health information — only transaction metadata.

## 8. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Account information | Until account deletion + 7 years for tax records |
| De-Identified Datasets in encrypted storage | Stored while listing is active; removed 30 days after delisting |
| Digital ledger records | Permanent (ledger immutability) |
| Consent records | Permanent (on ledger) + local copy retained |
| Contribution rewards/transaction history | 7 years (tax compliance) |
| Support communications | 3 years |

## 9. Your Rights

### 9.1 All Users
- **Access**: View all your Contribute Program data, listings, contribution rewards, and consent records via the Agent dashboard.
- **Revocation**: Revoke Contribute Program consent at any time, immediately delisting all active datasets.
- **Portability**: Export your contribution rewards history and consent records in machine-readable format.
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your XSpan account and associated non-ledger data.

### 9.2 California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)
- Right to know what personal information is collected, used, and shared.
- Right to delete personal information (subject to legal retention requirements).
- Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information. Note: De-Identified Datasets shared through the Contribute Program are not "personal information" under CCPA as they have been de-identified per HIPAA Safe Harbor.
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights.

### 9.3 Other State Privacy Rights
Residents of Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, and other states with consumer privacy laws may have additional rights. Contact legal@xspan.ai for state-specific requests.

## 10. Security

- Raw health data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) on your local device.
- De-Identified Datasets are encrypted before upload to secure storage.
- Wallet private keys are encrypted and stored in your OS keychain (macOS Keychain / Windows Credential Manager).
- The Contribute Program's infrastructure is audited by independent security firms.
- XSpan's cloud infrastructure is SOC 2 Type II compliant and operates under a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement.
