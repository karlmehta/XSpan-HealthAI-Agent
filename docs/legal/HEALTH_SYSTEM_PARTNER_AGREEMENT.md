**Date:** [DATE]

This Health System Partner Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by **XSpan, Inc.** ("XSpan") and the healthcare provider organization identified in the executed Order Form ("Health System Partner" or "Partner").

---

## 1. Overview

XSpan operates the XSpan Research Data Contribution Program, a platform where individuals contribute their own de-identified health data to verified Research Partners (pharmaceutical companies, research institutions, and technology companies) and earn contribution rewards.

This Agreement establishes a referral and revenue-sharing relationship where the Health System Partner introduces its patients to the XSpan HealthAI Agent platform. When referred patients choose to participate in the Contribute Program, the Partner receives a share of contribution rewards generated from those patients' data contributions.

**Critical distinction**: The Health System Partner does NOT provide or share patient data. Patients independently choose to (a) download the XSpan Agent, (b) connect their own health data sources, (c) de-identify their data locally, and (d) list it on the Contribute Program. The Partner's role is limited to referral and patient education.

## 2. Partner Obligations

### 2.1 Patient Referral
Partner may inform patients about the XSpan HealthAI Agent and Contribute Program through:
- Patient portal announcements
- Educational materials (provided by XSpan or co-developed)
- Discharge or visit summary inserts
- Provider-initiated conversations about health data ownership

### 2.2 Patient Autonomy
Partner shall:
- Clearly communicate that Contribute Program participation is **voluntary** and has no impact on clinical care
- NOT condition any aspect of care on Contribute Program participation
- NOT access, view, or influence individual patients' contribution listings, pricing, or rewards
- NOT require or pressure patients to participate

### 2.3 Compliance
Partner shall comply with all applicable laws including HIPAA, the 21st Century Cures Act, state health data privacy laws, and anti-kickback statutes. Partner acknowledges that the revenue share under this Agreement is for legitimate referral services and not for the sharing or referral of patient data by the Partner.

---

## 3. Revenue Share

### 3.1 Revenue Split for Partner-Referred Users

When a patient registers for the XSpan Contribute Program through a Partner referral link or attribution code, the following revenue split applies to all of that patient's contributions:

| Recipient | Percentage |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Patient (Data Contributor)** | **50%** |
| **Health System Partner** | **20%** |
| **XSpan** | **25%** |
| **Community Health Data Fund** | **5%** |

### 3.2 Attribution
- Partner referral is tracked via a unique Partner referral code or URL provided by XSpan.
- Attribution is assigned at the time of patient registration and is **permanent** for that patient's account.
- If a patient registers without a Partner referral code, the Partner does not receive a revenue share (the standard 50/45/5 split applies).

### 3.3 Payment Terms
- Revenue shares are calculated monthly based on confirmed Contribute Program transactions.
- XSpan will remit Partner's share within 30 days of month-end.
- Payment via ACH transfer or, at Partner's election, USD transfer to a designated account.
- Minimum payout threshold: $100 (amounts below threshold roll to next period).
- XSpan will provide monthly statements detailing: number of referred patients participating, total transactions, gross revenue, and Partner's share.

### 3.4 What Partner Does NOT See
- Individual patient identities linked to Contribute Program activity
- Individual patient contribution rewards, listings, or dataset contents
- Individual patient health data of any kind

Partner receives only **aggregate statistics**: total referred users participating, total transactions, and total revenue share earned.

## 4. XSpan Obligations

### 4.1 Platform Operation
XSpan shall:
- Operate the Contribute Program in compliance with applicable laws
- Maintain HIPAA-compliant de-identification standards (Safe Harbor method)
- Provide patient-facing educational materials about the Contribute Program
- Handle all Research Partner verification, data delivery, payment processing, and dispute resolution
- Provide Partner with a branded referral page and attribution tracking

### 4.2 Patient Support
XSpan shall provide direct support to patients regarding:
- Agent installation and setup
- Contribute Program consent and listing processes
- Contribution rewards and payout questions
- Consent revocation requests

### 4.3 Reporting
XSpan shall provide Partner with:
- Monthly aggregate Contribute Program participation and revenue reports
- Quarterly summary of Contribute Program growth, Research Partner activity, and trends
- Annual compliance certification regarding de-identification standards

## 6. Anti-Kickback Safe Harbor

The parties intend for this Agreement to comply with the personal services and management contracts safe harbor under 42 CFR 1001.952(d). Specifically:

- The Agreement is in writing and signed by the parties
- The Agreement specifies the services to be provided (patient referral and education)
- The compensation is set in advance (fixed percentage), consistent over the term, and reflects fair market value
- The services do not involve the counseling or promotion of a business arrangement that violates any state or federal law
- Revenue share is for legitimate referral services, not for the provision of patient data by the Partner

## 7. Term and Termination

### 7.1 Term
This Agreement is effective for an initial term of 12 months from the date of execution, automatically renewing for successive 12-month periods unless terminated.

### 7.2 Termination
Either party may terminate with 90 days' written notice. XSpan may terminate immediately if Partner breaches patient autonomy provisions (Section 2.2) or compliance obligations (Section 2.3).

### 7.3 Effect of Termination
- Upon termination, Partner ceases all referral activities.
- Patients who were previously referred retain their Partner attribution — the Partner continues to receive revenue share from those patients' future transactions for 12 months following termination, after which the attribution lapses and the standard split applies.
- XSpan will remit any outstanding revenue share within 60 days of termination.
