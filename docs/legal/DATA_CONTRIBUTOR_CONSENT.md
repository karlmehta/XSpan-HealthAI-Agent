## What is XSpan Contribute?

XSpan Contribute allows you to **contribute de-identified versions of your health insights** to verified research organizations such as pharmaceutical companies, research institutions, and technology companies. These Research Partners use de-identified health data to develop new treatments, improve clinical trials, advance medical research, and build health technology products.

Today, hospitals and intermediaries use your de-identified health data and earn billions of dollars — **you receive nothing**. XSpan Contribute changes this by letting you contribute your own data directly, advance medical research, and keep the majority of the contribution rewards.

## What Data Will Be Shared?

### Data That WILL Be Included (after de-identification)

You will choose which categories to include. Options are:

- **Cardiovascular**: Resting heart rate, heart rate variability, blood pressure (systolic/diastolic), VO2 max
- **Metabolic**: Fasting blood glucose, BMI (generalized), body fat percentage (generalized)
- **Sleep**: Total sleep time, deep/REM/light sleep duration, sleep efficiency, onset latency
- **Activity**: Daily steps, active minutes, exercise sessions per week, active calories
- **Nutrition**: 7-day averages for calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, water intake
- **Lab Results**: HbA1c, LDL/HDL cholesterol, triglycerides, TSH, vitamin D, ferritin, creatinine, eGFR, ALT/AST, CRP, homocysteine
- **Risk Scores**: Cardiovascular risk, metabolic risk, sleep disorder risk, burnout risk (computed scores, 0-100)
- **Genomics Risk Tiers**: Categorical risk levels (low/medium/high) for genetic risk factors — NOT raw genetic variants

### Data That Will NEVER Be Included

The following are stripped by the de-identification pipeline on your device and never leave your machine:

- Your name, email, phone number, or any contact information
- Your exact date of birth (replaced with 5-year age range, e.g., 30-34)
- Your address (replaced with first 3 digits of ZIP code only, or suppressed if population is small)
- Exact dates of any health readings (replaced with relative day offsets)
- Your XSpan user ID, medical record numbers, or device identifiers
- Free-text clinical notes, doctor names, or facility names
- Raw genetic variant data (SNP-level data is never shared; only categorical risk tiers)
- Any of the 18 HIPAA identifiers

### Preview Before You List

Before any data is listed on the Contribute Program, you will see a **full preview** of exactly which fields are included and their de-identified values. You must approve this preview before listing.

## Who Will Access My Data?

Research Partners must be **verified entities** that have completed KYC/KYB (Know Your Business) verification with XSpan. Typical Research Partners include:

- Pharmaceutical companies (drug development, clinical trial design)
- Biotech and life sciences companies
- Academic and government research institutions
- Health technology companies
- Public health agencies (e.g., CDC)

Research Partners agree to a strict Research Partner Access Agreement that **prohibits any attempt to re-identify you** from the de-identified data. Violation results in immediate termination and potential legal action.

**Individual consumers, insurance companies using data for coverage decisions, employers, and law enforcement are NOT permitted as Research Partners.**

## Your Rights

### You Can Revoke Consent at Any Time
- Click "Revoke Contribute Consent" in your Agent dashboard.
- All active listings are immediately removed.
- No new contributions of your data will occur.
- **Important**: Data from contributions that were already completed before revocation cannot be recalled. Research Partners who already obtained access to and received your data retain their license to use it. This is disclosed here so you can make an informed decision.

### You Can Update Your Listings at Any Time
- Add or remove data categories
- Change your price
- Pause listings temporarily without revoking consent

### You Can See Everything
- Full transparency into what data is listed, who accessed it (by pseudonymous identifier), and how much you earned
- Verification of all transactions on the secure digital ledger
- Audit log of every de-identification run performed by your Agent

## Risks and Limitations

### De-Identification Is Not Absolute
While the HIPAA Safe Harbor method is the legal standard for de-identification, and XSpan applies additional protections (k-anonymity, differential privacy, temporal de-correlation), no de-identification method can guarantee absolute anonymity against all theoretical re-identification attacks. The risk is extremely low but not zero, particularly as data analysis techniques evolve.

### Data Cannot Be Recalled After Delivery
Once a Research Partner has received and decrypted your De-Identified Dataset, XSpan cannot technologically force them to delete it. This is a fundamental limitation of any data sharing — once data is shared, it cannot be unshared.

### Contribution Rewards Are Not Guaranteed
The Contribute Program is a voluntary, market-driven platform. There is no guarantee that Research Partners will access your data, or that you will earn any specific amount.

### Tax Obligations
Contribution rewards are taxable income. XSpan will issue a 1099-MISC if you earn $600 or more in a calendar year. You are responsible for reporting contribution rewards to the IRS and any applicable state tax authority.
